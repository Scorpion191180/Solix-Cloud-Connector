- **Horse neigh** — [“Wiehern.ogg”](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wiehern.ogg)
  by Hü, released into the public domain.
- **Bactrian camel call** — [“Cri du chameau.ogg”](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cri_du_chameau.ogg)
  by J.P Longre, S. Mercier, M. Harry, P. Louisons, C. Ferry, G. Muys,
  K. Lens, B. Picard, V. Sandrilli, H. Van Hoeven, L. Rodriguez and

  [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- **Rottweiler barking** —
  [“Rottweiler Barking.oga”](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rottweiler_Barking.oga)
  by MichaeltheFox8621, licensed under
  [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- **Garden birds** —
  [“Birds singing in garden.ogg”](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Birds_singing_in_garden.ogg)
  by ezwa, released into the public domain.

The files are used unmodified. Playback volume, rate and excerpt length are
controlled by the web application at runtime.
